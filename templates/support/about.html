About Gigframe

Gigframe is a freelance marketplace platform built for Bangladesh's growing gig economy, connecting freelancers with clients through AI-assisted matching, transparent pricing guidance, and secure escrow-based payments.

Our Mission

We aim to make it easier for local freelancers to find meaningful work and get paid fairly, while giving clients a reliable, verified way to hire talent — all in one platform.

What Makes Gigframe Different

  • AI-powered semantic matching between freelancers and jobs
  • AI-assisted pricing suggestions based on real platform data
  • Escrow-based payments — funds are only released once work is approved
  • NID-verified freelancer identities for a trustworthy marketplace
  • Flexible accounts — switch between hiring and freelancing anytime

Gigframe was built as a practicum project, with the long-term vision of becoming a trusted home for freelance work in Bangladesh.
